# U02 World art direction: what a living ASCII scene looks like
Owner: astra (ultra)        Wave: U        Due: 01:00 Oman Oct 9
Goal: an art direction document for the procedural ASCII world that a builder can implement tonight: mood poses, animations, weather, time of day, celebrations, death and rebirth, each with an ASCII mockup at the real grid size.
Inputs: web/src/scene/pet.ts (the Truffle is lit ellipsoids, not pixel art: your mockups guide geometry and timing), web/src/scene/world.ts, decisions/0011, 0017, 0018, docs/01_product_spec.md (stages, moods, burrow, death), web/spike/ascii.html. Open the live app at https://truffle-web.ahmed-abied.workers.dev/demo in a browser and look at it (the chrome-devtools tools are allowed). Judge mode needs no pairing; the slider, midnight and heat buttons drive the real engine.
Deliverable: fleet/outbox/U02/RESULT.md and fleet/outbox/U02/storyboard.md.
Acceptance: for each of the 6 moods (content, affectionate, tired, wilting, just_woke, burrowed) and the 4 stages: a 24 x 14 ASCII mockup of the Truffle pose, a one-line description of the idle animation (what moves, period in seconds, amplitude in cells), and the blink and breath timing. For weather: rain, fog, storm, snow, wind at 10, 25, 45 km/h, each with a 40 x 12 mockup of the sky and ground treatment. For time: dawn, noon, dusk, night with stars and moon, each a note on palette intent (the palette file exists, say what to tune). For moments (decision 0017): one 2 to 4 s effect per kind, frames at 0, 1 and 2 s as 30 x 10 mockups. Death: gravestone scene. Rebirth: spore pushing up. A ranked list of the ten changes that add the most life for the least frame cost.
Do not: propose emoji, colour emoji, pixel sprites, or anything that needs Arabic inside the grid.
Report: RESULT.md with a summary and the ranked ten; storyboard.md with the mockups.
