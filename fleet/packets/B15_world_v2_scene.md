# B15 World v2: 30 fps and a living scene (decision 0018)
Owner: opus        Wave: night of Oct 8        Due: 03:00 Oman Oct 9
Goal: the ASCII world runs at 30 fps on a mid Android phone and looks alive: breathing, blinking, wind, rain, stars, shimmer, fireflies, birds, celebrations, death and rebirth.
Inputs: CLAUDE.md, decisions/0011, 0017, 0018, docs/01_product_spec.md (world section), web/src/scene/* (world.ts, pet.ts, raster.ts, palette.ts, astro.ts, sky.ts, grid.ts), web/src/fps.ts, web/src/main.ts (only to see how World is constructed and drawn; do not edit it beyond the minimum to wire a new constructor option if you must, and say so), web/spike/ascii.html, fleet/outbox/S08/RESULT.md, web/index.html and style.css for the layer markup.
Ownership fence: you own web/src/scene/*. B13 edits the rest of web/ at the same time. If you must touch main.ts or index.html, keep it to the smallest diff and list every line in RESULT.md.
Deliverable: code in web/src/scene/, tests, RESULT.md in fleet/outbox/B15/ with measured numbers.
Spec:
1. Speed first. Measure with `?fps=1` in Chrome with a mobile viewport (412 x 915) and 4x CPU throttling (the chrome-devtools tools are available to you, `npm run dev` serves the app; use the mock backend if the API needs credentials). Record the before numbers (fps, compose ms, paint ms). Then make it fast: cache static layers (sky, stars, ground) and recompute them only when the hour, weather or stage changes; recompute per frame only what moves; use typed arrays and avoid per-cell allocation; if DOM paint of stacked `<pre>` dominates, switch to one `<canvas>` with a glyph atlas (fillText once per glyph per colour into an atlas, drawImage per cell), keeping the existing colour palette and the same markup hooks so B13 can still read a frame for the share card (expose `world.frame(): { rows: string[]; colours: ... }` or similar and document it). Target 30 fps throttled; report the after numbers honestly even if short.
2. Life. Add, in this order, stopping when the time is up: Truffle breathing (slow squash), blinks every few seconds with a double blink sometimes, mood poses already there but stronger; grass or sand tufts bending with `windKmh`; rain as slanted streaks with splash ticks on the ground; stars twinkling and the moon phase readable; burrowed day: heat shimmer above the ground and a mound with a small hole where the Truffle was; fireflies after dark in grass countries; one or two birds crossing at dawn and dusk; the Elder stage visibly different (wider cap, a few spots).
3. Celebrations. `world.celebrate(kind: MomentKind, value: number): void` where MomentKind is exactly the union in decision 0017. Each kind gets a 2 to 4 s effect in the fx layer: stage_up a growth burst, best_day and beat_avg7 a few rising sparks, day_10k a wider burst, streak a ring of small sparks, lifetime a slow shower, heat_day_indoor a small cool ripple. Under reduced motion, show a single static frame of the effect for 2 s.
4. Death and rebirth. The gravestone scene gets a small wind-blown petal or two; the spore rebirth shows the new spore pushing up over 1 s.
5. Keep: reduced motion freezes the clock, Arabic never enters the grid, the grid stays 100 x 68, the layer and colour contract B13 and the share card rely on.
Acceptance: `cd web && npm test` green, `npm run build` clean, a before and after table of fps, compose ms and paint ms at 4x throttling, three screenshots (day, night with stars, burrowed) and one of a celebration in fleet/outbox/B15/. No deploy: Fable deploys.
Do not: edit files outside web/src/scene/* except as fenced above, add dependencies, add emoji anywhere, change the palette colours.
Report: RESULT.md with what was done, the numbers table, screenshots, what you did not get to.
