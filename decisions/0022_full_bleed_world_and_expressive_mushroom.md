# 0022 World first, with an expressive mushroom

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: directly requested by Ahmed during implementation.
This supersedes the 30 fps target and ball-like Spore rendering in 0018/0021.

The phone's main experience is an edge-to-edge ASCII world occupying most of the
screen, followed immediately by conversation. Move tools, demo controls,
keepsakes and settings to a secondary Pocket view. Keep the first screen calm.
The native Walk tab needs a separate visual pass: legible ASCII charts, obvious
period and source, clear totals, and secondary tracking controls.

Target 60 fps, measured on the actual Samsung as well as a throttled browser.
Report actual results; a target is not evidence. Keep a settled reduced-motion
frame, cached terrain and glyph atlas. Do not lower detail invisibly to claim
a better frame rate.

Truffle remains a full, recognizable mushroom even when young or asleep. Show
emotion through smooth changes in eyes, eyelids, mouth, gaze and posture.
Sleeping means closed eyes and gentle breathing, with no bouncing ball pose.
More intelligence/effort means more cap dots and a modest change in cap and base
size. These are visual signals only; energy tiers and stage rules do not change.

Keep authored gifts as small ASCII objects in the world. Tapping one opens its
note and art. Provide the same gift through a keyboard-accessible Pocket list.
Persist a person's explicitly chosen outing across a reload; returning can
acknowledge that intent but must not invent where they went or what they did.
No automatic model message or energy charge is introduced.
