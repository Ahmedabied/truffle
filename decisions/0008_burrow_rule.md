# 0008 - Burrow rule: heat days pause death and growth
Date: 2026-10-07. Proposed by Fable, approved by Ahmed.
When the daytime (06:00-22:00 local) max apparent temperature is >= 42C, Truffle burrows: no burn, zero_days frozen, steps feed energy but do not count toward growth, and the app suggests evening or indoor walks. Reason: Oman summers must not kill the pet for weather the user cannot control. The same rule covers extreme heat anywhere. Threshold tunable; S04 sanity-checks it.
