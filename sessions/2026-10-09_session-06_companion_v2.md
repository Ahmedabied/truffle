Completed October 9, 2026. Ahmed asked to act on the missing-feature audit and
run at least 25 Astra Ultra assignments. All 25 completed in waves of up to
three children beside root. Every assignment used Astra with ultra reasoning,
including the inherited-model child review. The runtime exposes no separate

## Decisions and resulting behavior

The architecture wave resolved food, companion and gift contracts. Root wrote
[decision 0023](../decisions/0023_continuous_food_and_living_companion.md) and
updated product/architecture specs before integration. It supersedes the prior
exploration-only status. Original v1 energy goldens remain, with new v2 rules
and migration tests rather than rewritten historical expectations.

- One fixed-point food balance carries through midnight; upkeep is 1,000 per
  actual day at all stages. Larger stores reward busy days without larger
  maintenance charges. Absolute effort thresholds make small walks useful.
  Requested conversations cost 20/60/200, charged once at first visible text.
  Protected reservations and cancellation preserve conservation.
- Heat pauses upkeep and the continuous empty-food clock. Death requires
  96 actual non-sheltered empty hours. Dated/pinned-zone absolute-total feeds,
  weather-outage shelter and replay protection remain enforced.
- Real server alarms compose fresh ASCII drawings while away, with authored
  bilingual notes and no paid generation or artificial food charge. One per
  pinned day and twelve retained, with owner/generation/request/job fences.
- Conservative English/Arabic outing, errand, rest and return recognition
  drives anticipation and movement happiness, respecting health/sleep priority.
  Native movement events require a verified current owner and document nonce.
- Android 0.4 improves period-matched Walk totals, missing-day coverage, large
  text and chart scrolling. Direct steps can request a coalesced feed and a
  fresh foreground expression. Reminders remain optional, off by default.
- The world fits its width without stretching glyphs. A complete mushroom
  sleeps with grounded feet, distinct faces and intelligence freckles. The
  latest message/reply pair remains visible. A direct first sample walk and
  Pocket disclosures address the independent blind UI review's largest issues.

Independent adversarial reviews found and repaired accounting timing,
reservation, delayed owner/state and gift-return races. The native refresh now
creates a fresh WebView rather than relying on hash navigation to re-run import.
Earlier reviews retain their original observations and scores; root did not
invent a higher blind-judge score after integration.

## Remaining boundaries

The Samsung stayed disconnected at the user's direction. Its historical
81-step result belongs to 0.3 and has no manually counted reference. Version
0.4 needs later physical ownership/reaction, notifications, TalkBack, battery,
endurance/reboot and sustained frame checks. No phone access is requested now.

Cross-tab visible presence remains uncoordinated; away-event delivery is best
effort. No new paid inference, training or model download ran. Provider totals
remain unreconciled against the $50 cap. Four/eight-second provider deadlines
are not total-latency measurements. Model-made gifts remain disabled. Same-base
model evaluation and human Arabic preference limits remain explicit.

article stays `published: false`; no article or external message was sent.

not staged. MemPalace KG query and final promotion both failed with
