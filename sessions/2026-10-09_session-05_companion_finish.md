## New energy request

Ahmed asked for subagents to explore useful energy consumption and food surviving
midnight. Two independent Astra specialists examined actual engine behavior,
compared alternatives and challenged each other's conclusions. Root reran the
isolated harness: **45 exploratory checks passed**; the specialist also ran
**72 unchanged production engine/feed/time tests**.

Current energy already carries, but midnight stage burn discards a late small
feed, cap losses waste overflow, and an Elder fed 7,000 daily can still die after
four zero-midnight snapshots. The recommendation is gentle continuous consumption
at a provisional 1,000 per elapsed 24 hours for every age, one balance, no midnight
charge. A 10k Elder day with ten replies and a full rest day ends at 7,840 under the
proposal, versus zero currently. These are synthetic budget simulations, not
exercise targets or measured biology. Optional bounded storage saves overflow;
a fixed 1k chat floor was rejected because it blocks a first small conversation.

Useful spending is real requested conversation, explanation and planning.
Animation/authored gifts stay free. Memory consent, death versus dormancy, growth
tier changes and ordinary versus deep effort are explicit follow-up decisions.
No live balance, production engine or golden case was changed by this research.
Migration must preserve owners/history, avoid double settlement and reserve
in-flight chat costs. Historical unsent steps require a separate per-day ledger;
they must not bypass today's anti-replay checks.
