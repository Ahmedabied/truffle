# 01 - Competitive landscape (Week 1, read on Oct 7 afternoon)

14 entries under #hf26challenge by mid-afternoon Oct 7 (day 3 of 7).

## The pile

About 9 entries are the same product: an offline Gemma outdoor companion or scavenger hunt. GrassQuest, GrassGuide, Grounded, TrailMind AI, TrailCard, NatureQuest, GreenQuest AI, TouchGrass AI, Touch Grass Bingo. Most are 2 - 3 minute reads. Several have no live link. GrassQuest published with `[ADD YOUR OUTDOOR STORY HERE]` still in the post. Most never went outside despite the theme asking for it.

## The one to beat

**Sobuj Ghonta (The Green Hour)** by Sayem Rahman. Bangla + English PWA, Gemma 4 26B-A4B (with a local gemma2:9b option), Open-Meteo hourly data, OpenStreetMap, Meta MMS-TTS, Express + Vite, live on Render with a zero-key instant demo for Dhaka, real field test in Ramna Park. 7-minute read. 5 reactions (highest). Enters Gemma + Render.

What it does well: bilingual with a culturally rooted name, real data, live demo that needs no setup, actual field test. What it lacks: a character, a reason to come back, a hard technical idea beyond integration.

## What DEV judges have rewarded before

From the Passion Edition winners post: the overall winner built a 3D walking guide to a Seoul district from 3,535 real OpenStreetMap footprints with a guide named Tobaggi (a Korean word for someone born in a neighbourhood who never left). The judges' summary dwelt on the name and the feeling. Other winners: a horror desktop that needs headphones, a message-to-exoplanets app, a World Cup "pick your side" machine grounded in search. Pattern: personal hook, named character with roots, real data, one hard technical thing, written with feeling. Reaction counts varied from 1 to 32; they only break ties.

## Our angle, by contrast

A creature you raise, not a guide you consult. A name with roots (Truffle / فقع). Real steps, real weather, real growth numbers. One hard idea: the model's effort is gated by energy we compute, with personality in fine-tuned open weights. A three-day diary from a place where "touch grass" is a seasonal luxury.
