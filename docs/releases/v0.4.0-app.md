# Truffle 0.4.0: debug test prerelease

Truffle's Android companion gets a clearer walking notebook and fresh movement
reactions. The embedded world now uses continuous food and server-created ASCII
gifts.

- **Walk:** Today, 7 days and 30 days show matching totals, dates and coverage.
  Missing native records stay blank; recorded zero stays distinct. Large text
  and long totals remain readable with scrolling.
- **World:** Fresh direct-counter steps can change the verified pet's expression
  while World is visible. The reaction itself adds no food. Reload World creates
  a fresh document while retaining the native owner and connection settings.
- **Food:** Stored food carries into tomorrow, with continuous upkeep and a
  bounded reserve. Heat shelter pauses upkeep and the empty-food clock.
- **Gifts and conversation:** Accepted away plans can produce procedural ASCII
  gifts through server alarms, with no model call or food charge. Everyday and
  Think deeper controls make conversation effort explicit. The sample demo
  labels its authored replies and simulated charges.

The APK requires Android 9 or newer. Pause direct counting before upgrading,
install over the existing app signed with the same key to retain its pet and
diary, then resume explicitly from Walk. See the [Android setup guide](https://github.com/Ahmedabied/truffle/tree/main/feeder-android).
